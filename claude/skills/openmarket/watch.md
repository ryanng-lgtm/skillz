# om watch

<!-- Changing this? See docs/evals.md (compose, tier 3). -->

A watch is a source plus its steps. The daemon reads every source, writes one row per event into the watch's history, and runs the steps on each row: an ai step reads and answers, a tool step runs one frozen function, a money step moves money; steps chain into one another, and every chain is armed by one human yes.

### Guardrails

- One noun, one verb family: `watch_*` tools and `om watch ...` commands. There are no alerts, signals, strategies or schedules any more; a market condition is a watch whose source is `condition`, a trading rule is a money step reading a verdict or its watch's own condition level, a cadence is a `timer` source (§"Sources", §"Actions").
- One watch may present several sources (`sources[]`): the user sees ONE watch with sources and chains; speak of its sources, never of the watches underneath (§"Upstream source").
- Three step kinds: `ai`, `tool`, `money`. Delivery is the watch's `notify` setting, never a step; `webhook` is the outbound door (§"Actions").
- A money step is the user's authorization to move money on every fire, unattended, from the moment its chain is armed. Author one only when the user asked for the trade or the transfer; a notification ask carries no money step. The recipe carries hints only; the amounts (`size`, `capital`, `to`, `amount`, `price`) are typed by the human on the arm card, never by you (§"Money step").
- The card is the ask. Every step lands DISABLED; one card per chain lists every step (the money line whole, the prompt in full) and one yes arms all of them; a no leaves the whole chain off. Never pass `authorization` yourself; the runtime strips it. A chain with a money step arms at the installing machine's terminal only: in chat, `watch_arm` returns the command to run; over MCP every chain arm is refused (§"Arming").
- An end-to-end setup or activation request calls `watch_arm` for its chains in the same turn (`watch_resume` for a source-only watch): the card obtains approval. Do not ask for a separate chat yes or wait for the user to say enable. A request to build it and leave it off keeps the draft paused.
- Say "disabled until armed" and name `om watch arm <watch> <chain>` until the card came back; never "armed".
- Steps that touch belong to one chain and arm together; chains of one watch arm independently. An edit to any step, box, pin or delivery stands its chain down until it is armed again (§"Arming").
- Limits are pinned: 32 steps per watch, one exposure-creating money step per chain (a `cancel_order` that targets the chain's order rides beside it), one strategy step per watch, one producer per step, chain depth 8, fan-in of up to 100 sources (§"Actions").
- Step ids are identity and immutable; naming an id already on the watch replaces that step in place (its readers keep their producer). Every step also has a NAME (`payload.label`, 1 to 60 chars, unique on the watch): what the delivery header, `om watch history`, `om watch show`, /notis and every card print for the step instead of its id. Give every step one when you author it; a step left unnamed is named from what it does (`dip or noise`, `metric_get on BTCUSDT`, `buy ETH 100`), and `om watch action rename` names it later without standing its chain down (§"Actions").
- What never leaves the machine: box values, notify settings, channels, enabled flags, the inbound door token, the sealed model, run rows, delivery receipts and an ai step's reads. A published pack carries the sources, the steps, the hints, the caps and the dependency pins (§"Doors").
- A watch's history is on-demand context, never live venue state: prices, positions, balances and probabilities come from the live market and venue tools.
- A vendor news feed id is not an OpenMarket registry address. Discover it through `news_catalog`; author a Fast alert through the listener source on `watch_create` after `schema_read {intent: "listener_create"}` (§"Sources"). Use returned stream refs for held feeds, never invent one.
- Model use has one control: the judge model (`agent.judge_model`) and the user's own key. Never invent a read cap or a budget; a provider limit is a judge fault shown in words and re-judged on the provider's timing (§"Model use").
- A complete instruction dispatches: never ask "OK to save?" in prose before `watch_create` or a lifecycle verb. Where a card is required, raising it IS asking.
- MCP cannot arm a chain (`mcp_cannot_arm_chain`, armed capital window or not); `watch_arm`, `watch_action_remove`, `watch_remove` and `watch_page_add` are withheld there by name. A chat card or the `om watch` CLI are the only doors, and money chains arm at the terminal alone.
- Out of scope is answered honestly with the closest supported watch; never invent a workaround and never emit a spec the daemon rejects.
- A save is judged on the watch's OWN paths (what it reads, the readers that depend on it, cycles through it). Another watch's own dangling reference is `roster_warnings` on the result (`{watch, code, message}`), never a refusal: relay it once (`om watch remove <watch>` clears a reader nobody wants, or add its source again) and never retry the save because of it (§"Errors").

### Routing

- "Alert me when BTC funding goes negative", "ping me the moment BTC crosses 100k", "BTC drops 5% in an hour" → a `condition` source on `watch_create`, opened by the composer's market-rule handoff (§"Condition source"); "the moment" / "as soon as" → `latency_class: "fast"`.
- "Buy $250 of BTC under 90k" (a TRIGGER, one moment) → a condition watch carrying one `money` step in `order` mode with `terms.side` set and no producer, `max_fires: 1` by default; the size is a box the human types at the arm (§"Money step").
- "A bot", "trade this rule for me", "long while RSI is oversold" (a REGIME, a state to hold through) → one watch with a `money` step in `strategy` mode, paper by default, reading either an ai step declaring `output: {type: "verdict"}` (or a `metric_rule` tool step) or — when the state is the watch's own condition — that source's level with no input (§"Strategy mode"). Truly unable to tell trigger from regime → ONE structured question with those two options and nothing else.
- "When it fires, decide whether to buy" → an ai step with `output: {type: "word", words: ["buy", "sell", "none"]}` feeding an `order` money step through `input` (§"AI step", §"Money step").
- A URL alone ("watch this page") → `watch_page_add` (`om watch <url>`), the kit's page door with the URL bound; a URL inside a sentence ("check <url> every N minutes, when it changes do X") is the composer's, which hands off the page with its cadence; the change clause is a `watch_action_add` ai step on the page watch once it exists (the kit's change door), armed by its own card (§"Page source").
- Any watch request in the person's own words ("watch <subject> for me", "tell me whenever <subject> posts about <topic>", a number om does not stream with a level or a cadence, a rule on a market om streams) → `watch_kit`, then ONE `watch_compose` call with the sentence the kit bound (§"Watch a subject"): one parse places every clause and hands off each clause it cannot place with the verb and the one shape allowed (§"Build from a description"). Combining saved watches: §"Combine existing watches".
- A brief, a reminder or a run on a clock ("every morning at 8 do X"), and a question the model answers on a cadence ("ask the model every hour whether ..."), are sentences: the composer places them or hands them off with the shape allowed (`watch_create` with a `timer` source, §"Timer source"; `watch_model_add` when the person names the model as the reader, §"Model source").
- "When watch Z lands a row, screenshot my chart" → an `upstream` source on that watch with a `chart_screenshot` tool step (§"Upstream source", §"Tool step").
- A step over a FOLDER ("summarize the <folder> daily") is a sentence naming the folder: the composer's timer handoff reads its members (the folder's own watch: one `timer` trigger plus every member as a `role: "context"` entry, §"Upstream source"). A step for one member goes on that member's watch (`watch_action_add`, the kit's change door). A folder's sends ("send the <folder> to discord") → `watch_edit` with `select_group` naming the folder and the notify change (§"Edit a watch").
- "When a page posts OR the indicator reads above 1 OR every 24h, do X" is a sentence: the composer's, each source handed off with its shape; ONE `watch_create` with `sources[]` (one entry per source) and the steps is the terminal's and the form's shape, the user seeing one watch (§"Upstream source").
- "Watch my inbox / my CI / a webhook / a log pipe" → an `inbound` source; the token the create returns is what pushes rows in (`skill_read("connect-source")`).
- "Build me a watch that ..." (a sentence naming triggers, context, filters, steps and a destination), "set it up end to end", "compose it" → §"Build from a description": discover, one draft, refusals resolved, validated, one card, armed, then published on request. A news feed beside a price rule is context, not a second trigger (§"Trigger and context roles").
- Writing or editing an ai step's prompt, `tools`, `output` or `definitions`, and the refusals `watching_unknown_source` / `watching_unknown_tool` / `watching_reader_unsatisfied` / `watching_definitions_overlap` → `skill_read("watch-prompts")`: the header the step sees, INPUT per source kind, the tool menu with return shapes, `@{Name}` mentions (§"Mentions"), worked prompts, refusal-is-the-fix.
- "What am I watching / is it armed / what is it doing" → `watching_overview`; "why is watch 3 broken" → `watch_stats` with the id; "show me its errors" → `watch_history` with `kind: "error"` (§"Read a watch").
- "Arm it" / "turn the trade on" → `watch_arm` with the watch and the chain (the chat card arms a chain with no money step; a money chain returns the terminal command) (§"Arming").
- "Raise those approval cards" → `watch_kit` opens the arm door; use each identified watch and chain id for `watch_arm`, and ask which chain if the reference is unclear (§"Arming").
- "Pause / resume / delete watch 3" → `watch_pause` / `watch_resume` / `watch_remove` by id. A combined watch takes its unshared sources with it; `ids` selects exact watches, `group` selects the set. Cards: §"Lifecycle".
- "Run / retry this failed watch again now" → `watch_retry`; it releases unowned classifier backoff immediately. `watch_resume` is only for a paused watch. Cards: §"Lifecycle".
- "Fix my watches", "fix my h2h watch", "retry the broken ones", "do that fix" → `watch_kit` first: a watch named in the words (a whole name, or a word of one in a sentence that asks for a change) opens the change door with `watch_retry`, `watch_repair` and `watch_stats` on it; no watch named lists this home's broken watches under `broken` (the repair door) with the same verbs attached, one call per watch meant; words fitting several watches come back under `candidates` (the which door): ask the person which one, never guess, and stop. A change said with no word of the watch's name ("fix the counter-strike one", "the noisy one") goes through the composer like any sentence: its reader names the watch from this home's list and the result's `changes` rows plus `next` carry the change door on it (a row with `candidates` is the same question for the person).
- "Change watch 3 to 4500" → `watch_edit` with the new `source.condition`; "change the size to 50" → `watch_edit` with `boxes`, then the chain re-arms (§"Edit a watch").
- "No rankings", "less fluff", "too noisy", "why did this get through", "only Z from S", "tune <watch>", "not this", "keep this kind" → `watch_tune`, the yes through `watch_tune_apply` (§"Tune a watch").
- "Change the prompt the package shipped" → `watch_action_add` naming that step's id (it becomes yours, kept across updates); "take the author's version back" → `watch_edit` with `take` (§"Edit a watch"); the whole installed-package customizing arc is `skill_read("marketplace", section = "Watch packs")`.
- Share or publish a watch or a set of watches ("publish my watch", "send this watch to followers", "broadcast this", "let people follow this", "publish the desk as one package", "sell it", "share it into #desk"), "stop broadcasting" (`watch_pause`), follow, install, fork, mute, knocks → §"Doors".
- A typed refusal (`money_needs_terminal`, `chain_digest_drift`, `watching_reader_unsatisfied`, `watch_spec_version`, ...) → §"Errors" for the one recovery each.
- Plotting a watch's rows on a chart → `skill_read("chart-actions", section = "Pins")`; would-have-fired replays and event studies → `skill_read("research")`.

### Reply shape

One line saying what is watched and which chains are armed or waiting, in the user's words with every default named; a condition watch quotes its readings as one clause per leg; the delivery as one row; venues and metrics are words, never registry ids. An arm card is the ask; no closing offer.

## Build from a description

From a sentence to a watch the user can approve: the procedure, the shape per ask, the defaults, the card, the arm; the kit serves this, read it before any watch ask.

**Choose the door.** The kit does, from the person's own words: a request in words goes to `watch_compose`; a pasted feed, handle or URL alone goes to its named-source door (`watch_page_add`, `watch_create`) with the literal bound; a market rule is a sentence and reaches `watch_create` only through the composer's handoff. For the desk's request, quote the person's message(s), exactly as typed in any turn of this conversation; om verifies it and reads the whole matched messages with this turn's words. When the person answers your last message, quote that offer; a turn with only a yes must quote the offer. A rewrite matches nothing: om reads this turn's words alone and returns a note; if those words name nothing buildable, ask the person to type the watch in one sentence.

**A handoff.** A clause the composer cannot place comes back under unplaced with its verb and its build, the one shape that verb may build for it (a rule on a market om streams; a timer with its rhythm, what it reads and whether it may search the web; a page; a search; a question for the model; a step), and the result's `next` block carries the call with the arguments the build settles. Make that call in that shape and no other: every other build from chat is refused with the road named.

**The procedure, one turn.**

1. Read what the draft needs, silently: `markets` for a market, `metric_list` for an indicator id (`wrun/...`), `watch_tools` before naming an ai step's `tools`, `watch_show` on a watch the user names. A level with no side ("alert me at RSI 30") takes one `metric_get` first: the current value picks `crosses_above` or `crosses_below`, because an edge armed on the wrong side of the value is already past its crossing and stays silent.
2. A request in words uses ONE `watch_compose` carrying the sentence the kit bound. A named source (a bound handle, a handed-off rule or timer) uses ONE `watch_create` carrying `label`, `source` (one) or `sources[]` (several; each entry is a source arm plus its `name`, `role` (trigger unless said) and `enabled`; a listener takes `display_name`, an inbound entry is named after the watch), `notify`, `actions[]`; pass `enabled: false` when steps ride it. A page URL, a hosted search or a model question uses its own door (`watch_page_add`, `watch_search_add`, `watch_model_add`, each with `enabled: false` where it takes one; a URL alone from the kit, the rest from a handoff): with `watch` naming a saved watch, the door appends its sealed entry to that file; alone, it creates a one-source watch.
3. The result's `card` is the end state: show it verbatim in a code block. A typed refusal is the next edit, never a question (§"Errors").
4. Steps waiting (`chains_to_arm`)? Call `watch_arm` with `chains_to_arm[0]` in the SAME turn: the card prints the whole watch (what triggers it, every step, where it goes) and the user's yes arms it. The card's "No, tell the agent what to do instead" is the edit path: change the draft (`watch_edit` for the label, source, delivery, a prompt or a box; `watch_action_add` naming a step's `id` replaces it; `watch_source_add` / `watch_source_remove`), show the new card, arm again. Two chains, two cards. A chain with a money step never arms from chat: the result names `om watch arm <watch> <chain>`; say it.
5. No steps? The watch runs from the create (`enabled` defaults to true): show the card and say in one line what it watches and where it posts.
6. Ask only what changes what the watch means: a level the user left out, a channel when several are paired and none is default, a door or a price on a share, the venue and side of a trade. Never ask about a default; name it in the outcome line and let the next message change it.

**What runs on each row.** `actions[]` on the create (or `watch_action_add` later), every step `{id?, kind, input?, channels?, payload}` with `payload.label` (its name, 1 to 60 chars, unique) on every kind:

- `ai`: `payload: {label, prompt, output, tools?, definitions?, recommended_model?, max_runs_per_day?}`. `output` is `{type: "text"}` (default), `{type: "word", words: [...]}` (one lowercase word from the list; a `definitions` entry per word says what makes the model answer it, `unsure` names the fallback) or `{type: "verdict"}` (long / short / flat with confidence). `tools` absent = every read plus web search, `[]` = none, a list = exactly those from `watch_tools`. Mention a source as `@{Name}` exactly as named. The prompt's header, the leash and worked prompts: `skill_read("watch-prompts")`.
- `tool`: `payload: {label, action, args}`: one frozen function on every row (`chart_screenshot`, a read, `metric_rule` for a verdict without a model).
- `money`: `payload: {label, mode, terms, boxes: {<box>: {hint}}, caps: {max_fires, max_size?, expires_at?}}`, modes `order` (box `size`), `strategy` (box `capital`, paper by default), `transfer` (`to`, `amount`), `purchase` / `cancel_subscription` (`price`), `cancel_order`. Author one only when the user asked for the trade or the transfer; amounts are boxes the human types on the arm card, never values you write. An `order` reads `buy | sell | none` from its producer (or a sealed `terms.side`); a `strategy` reads a verdict, or on a condition watch no input at all and that source's own level (§"Money step", §"Strategy mode"). A TRIGGER ("buy $250 of BTC under 90k") is a condition plus an `order`, `max_fires: 1`; a REGIME ("trade this rule for me", "long while RSI is oversold") is a `strategy` — reading a verdict step, or reading a condition source's level with no input at all.

## Condition source

The typed market condition: the tree, its metrics and operators, fire mode and cooldown; read this when composing a condition or reading one back.

The arm is a deferred part on `watch_create` and `watch_edit`: `schema_read {intent: "condition_create"}` loads its full schema; once loaded, the condition grammar is written at the `condition` field (the stub carries the example only).

A condition is a tree over metric leaves: a single leaf `{metric, selector, op, value, params?}`, `all` / `any` / `not` combinators, a `Compare` of two sides (`{left, op, right}`, where each side is a metric reference or an arithmetic `expr` such as `multiply`), or a `script` leaf naming a body under `~/.openmarket/scripts/` (§"Script condition"). `selector` names the market as `market: "EXCHANGE:SYMBOL"` (or the pair `exchange` + `symbol`; the stored watch keeps the pair), `interval` and optionally `quote`; a Polymarket selector's symbol is the 66-char `conditionId`, never the group slug.

Metrics: `price`, `delta_pct` (`params.bars`), `delta_abs`, `volume`, `volume_sma` (`params.period`), `funding_rate`, `open_interest`, `open_interest_delta_pct`, `rolling_high` / `rolling_low` (`params.bars`), `rsi`, `sma`, `ema`, `macd*`, `bb_*`, `atr`, `stoch_*`, `wma`, `zscore`, plus any installed `wrun/@scope/name/output` (a published pack pins that package as a dependency: §"Doors"). Operators: `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`, `eq` compare a level; `crosses_above` and `crosses_below` fire on the tick the value crosses and require `selector.interval`. An edge never sits under `not`. A Compare with an edge needs a metric reference on BOTH sides on the SAME interval (golden cross `sma(50)` x `sma(200)`, breakout `price` x `rolling_high`, spike `volume` x `multiply(3, volume_sma)`). A leaf whose lookback exceeds the data cap is refused at create; one already saved over it reads unavailable on its own, so a healthy sibling under `any` still fires and the refused leaf names itself in `last_error`.

`fire_mode` is `recurring` (default: fires whenever TRUE, rate-limited by `cooldown`, default 60s) or `once` (fires and pauses itself). `act_within` (top level of the watch, same grammar as `cooldown`) is the late fence for EVERY row of the watch (a relayed fire, a local fire, a page or feed update, an inbound push): an update older than this when it reaches the steps is journaled as `late` and never acted on (no step, no money, no forward), and the owner is told once per gap with the cause (`your daemon was off` or `late on the way`); default = cooldown (at least 1m), else 5m; `act_within: null` on edit clears it back. An order or strategy step may carry its own `payload.act_within`, narrowing only (default the smaller of the watch's and 5m; a wider value refuses `wider than the watch's <v>; raise the watch's first`). A timer watch has no fence unless it names one. The author ships the fence with the pack and the installer edits it (§"Doors"). `latency_class: "fast"` arms the push-stream lane for "the moment" asks; omit it otherwise and say which lane armed. `expires_at` retires the watch after an instant. The create result carries `readings[]` (each leaf's current value) and a `routing_note`: quote both.

```json
{
  "label": "BTC funding negative",
  "source": {
    "kind": "condition",
    "condition": {
      "metric": "funding_rate",
      "selector": { "exchange": "BINANCE_FUTURES", "symbol": "BTCUSDT", "interval": "HOUR" },
      "op": "crosses_below",
      "value": 0
    },
    "cooldown": "1h"
  },
  "notify": { "enabled": true, "channel": "telegram" }
}
```

Catch-up: a trigger that landed while the daemon was down is reported late as a closed-bar fire in the gap digest; a money step never runs on a catch-up row, and any row past the watch's `act_within` when it reaches the steps is `late` the same way.

## Script condition

A condition whose leaf is an operator-authored script: what it reaches and why it is terminal-only; read this before mentioning one.

A `{kind: "script", script: "<name>"}` leaf spawns a body under `~/.openmarket/scripts/` on every tick as the operator's user, unsandboxed, with `om` first on its PATH: it can place orders and read every stored key. ## Page source

`watch_page_add` turns a URL into a watch: the probe, the page terms the card freezes, the region the setup read anchors; read this for "watch this page".

`om watch <url>` (`watch_page_add`) probes the URL for reachability and watches it as the page it is: the card freezes the URL, the cadence, whether the model reads (`model_read`, default true; false is diff only, no model reads), the provider and model, and the reads line; nothing is created until the yes. Always propose `label`: two to five plain words in the user's own words for what this watch is, never the page's title and never its address; the user keeps or changes it on the card. Left out, the watch is named by its address, or by `group` when that folder does not exist yet. After the yes the setup read anchors the region that matters and writes the notes (`anchor_heading`, `focus_note`, `item_definition` in the reply); only that region is compared on later checks, so chrome, ads and rotating banners outside it never count. No feed is looked for unless `prefer: "feed"`: then exactly one live feed becomes a free feed watch (no model read, no card) and several answer `choice_required` with `feed_candidates` to pass back as `feed_choice`; a URL alone goes through this door, which tells a feed from a page itself; `watch_create`'s `feed` listener is for a URL known to be a feed, and the write probes it first and refuses a web page (`watch_feed_url_is_page`, naming the feed the page declares when it has one), a gone address (`watch_feed_url_gone`) and a document that is not a feed (`watch_feed_url_not_feed`); an X handle is probed the same way (`watch_x_handle_gone`), a rule's market is looked up (`watch_market_not_listed`), and a one-shot `at` must still be ahead (`watch_timer_in_past`). A change clause ("when it changes do X") is a `watch_action_add` ai step on the created watch with the instruction as `payload.prompt`; it lands disabled and its chain is armed by its own card. `focus_note`, `item_definition` and `anchor` on the `page` block narrow what counts as an item; `mode: "page"` (default) or `"document"`. A page joins a watch with several sources through its own door first, then as an `upstream` entry (§"Upstream source"). Several pages go in ONE call as `urls` (1 to 100, each page once), never one call per URL: every page is fetched once now with no model, one card lists them all (four rows, then `and N more`; a page that cannot be read is a `Skipped: N` row with its reason, never dropped), the yes freezes exactly the pages the card showed, and every other field (`intent`, `cadence_sec`, `daily_cap`, `notify`, `group`, `watch`) applies to every page. The reply's `pages` rows and `counts` name every page added and every page skipped with its reason (`10 requested, 9 added, 1 skipped`), the counts and notes ahead of the rows; a receipt too long for one reply carries each URL's short form on the rows and names the file under the home (`receipt_path`) that holds every row whole; a skipped row marked `transient` (a timeout, a 429, a 5xx, a host that did not resolve) is worth adding again later. `urls` never discovers feeds and never joins a standing read: those stay one-URL calls.

From the form (`enabled: false`) a URL a live page watch already reads (enabled, the same URL and mode, its authority active) JOINS that watch's stream by default (`join`; `false` runs a fresh read): no setup read, no new authority, no terms of its own. The reply is `status: "joined"` with `joined` (the watch and its label, `region`, `item_definition`, the stream's `cadence_sec` and `daily_cap` after the merge, `provider`, `model`) and the note `Joined the read '<label>' stands on <host/path>: no setup read, no terms of its own.`; the terms merge monotonically onto the stream (the faster cadence, never under 60 s; the higher cap) and the notes name what applied (`The cap asked (5) is under the stream's 20: a shared stream keeps the higher cap, and its reads are this home's.`). A fresh read whose recipe a live read already holds joins that stream too (one active authority per URL, mode and recipe). Enabled calls (the agent and CLI doors) never join: they adopt the standing watch as before. Say "joins <label>" and the shared terms; a cap-0 ask on a paid stream joined that paid stream, never "no model requests".

## Number source

A page listener in `reading` mode reads a number a publisher serves as JSON on a clock and runs its rule in om; read this for a watch the composer made from a number.

A reading source is the page listener with `mode: "reading"`. Its sealed recipe names the publisher, up to 8 numeric fields by JSON path (each with its key, its name in the person's words and its unit), an optional `rule` over those keys in the condition grammar's own operators (`gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`, `eq`, `crosses_above`, `crosses_below`) and the grammar's own `cooldown` string for a level rule that keeps holding. Every check reads the sealed fields into ONE row carrying `values` (kind `reading`, its title the reading line `<name> <value>`), never a row per changed path and never a model call; every check stores its row (the reading is a series a chart can plot), and the rule runs in om over it, the previous reading read off the page baseline, deciding only what goes out: the reading that meets the rule is the alert and goes at once whatever the send cadence; a plain reading is held for the watch's mark, and stays quiet on a watch with a rule and no mark; a crossing alerts once and a level re-alerts on its cooldown. Only `watch_compose` makes one: the parse places the number, the scout asks who publishes it as JSON, om fetches each candidate itself and keeps the first whose field resolves to one finite number, and the card shows the publisher, the value om read now, the rule or the cadence and the delivery; there is no hand door for it (`watch_page_add` never writes this mode), and a number nobody publishes as JSON comes back as the composer's timer handoff (`watch_create` with a timer at the reading's cadence and one ai step that may search the web), never as a page or a search door. What it sends: a rule fires each time it is met, and the first message after the yes is the reading itself; an every-N-hours or daily cadence sends the newest reading at the mark as the fixed line `<name> <value> · <HH:MM>` with no writer call; no rule and no cadence is recorded only (`notify.enabled: false`), said on the card. `f` on the panel fetches now and prints the value line and the rule's verdict (`met` or `not met`, no model request); its `new` count is the rows stored, never the fetch's own. A publisher that stops answering is the lane's own wait; a JSON body missing the sealed field twice in a row is `reading_lost`, said on the status line: ask om for the number again, never an automatic paid re-scout.

## Combine existing watches

Make one watch from saved watches; read this when the user wants their existing logs together.

`om watch combine <group or ids...> --name <name>` (`watch_combine` with `watches` and `name`) creates one watch reading 2 to 100 existing watches. One approval names every source that stops pinging and the new watch that pings. The sources keep their logs; the combined log fills in the background and folds repeated stories. Name that one watch to read, with its sources underneath.

Refuse while a source's opening read or link checks are unfinished, when a source already belongs to a combined set, or when the name is in use. Let pending reads and checks finish; otherwise choose free sources or a new name. Publishing a combined set is refused: `Local use only: it reads watches on this machine`. A new source that would make a loop is refused before saving.

## Model source

A prompt a model answers on a cadence with the tools you tick on the menu, every finding a row on the user's own credential; read this for "ask the model every hour ..." asks.

`watch_model_add` (`om watch model-add "<prompt>" --group <label> --every <duration>`) adds a model run as a source of a watch (a named one, or a new one-source watch). The prompt is the filter: what to look for, in the user's own words, up to 4,000 characters, printed whole on the card. The daemon runs it on the cadence, on the installer's own model credential, and every finding lands as a source row like a feed item (deduped by the finding's url, else a digest of its text), so the filter, the steps, the delivery, the history and the sharing of any watch apply to it unchanged. `output` is `rows` (the default: zero or more findings a run) or `answer` (exactly one text answer a run, or none). The model run and the ai step are ONE primitive with one menu (§"AI step" for the step's side): `tools` absent seals the default set (every read on this home plus web search), `[]` seals none, a list seals exactly those names; `watch_history` on the list is bound to the watch itself, so the prompt can skip what it already reported; a reaching row (`web_research`, `page_read`, `search_files`, `make_image`) is one isolated request each on the sealed lane. Member creation, never an edit: a changed prompt, cadence, tools or answer shape is a new run, or an edit on the same card (`watch_edit`, below). Never on MCP. Writing the prompt: `skill_read("watch-prompts", section = "A source prompt is not a step prompt")`.

The card (mandatory, never auto; one shape in chat, at the terminal and on install), the lines as they appear in the approval, the dangerous parts whole and first:

```text
Model run: every 1 h, on <provider/model> (<whose credential>); author recommends <provider/model>: <reason>
Prompt: "<the prompt, whole>"
Tools: everything OpenMarket can read (incl. your positions and balances), Search the web and X, Make images (grok-imagine-image) (up to 20 calls a run; each search, file or image call is one more request)
Reaches out: web + X search on this model; images on xAI; what it searches for may carry what this run has read
Answer: rows (zero or more findings a run)
Ceiling: up to 24 runs a day (your cap: 10); the probe now counts as the first
```

- **The menu.** Every row a person can tick, plain (`watch_tools` returns them with `plain`, `when`, `group`, `default`, `spend`, `warn`, `needs`, `runs_on`, plus `default_set` and `not_offered`): the master row `Everything OpenMarket can read` (prices, indicators, lookups, the news journal, this watch's own past rows, your positions and balances; each read row unticks on its own), the reaching rows `Search the web (and X on Grok)` (`web_research`, on by default), `Read a web page by address` (`page_read`), `Search files you uploaded to the maker` (`search_files`, the store id typed on the row and sealed: `file_store`), `Make images` (`make_image`, the image model picked on the row and sealed: `image_model`, `image_size`, `image_quality`), and the greyed rows this build never ticks (`run_code`, `mcp_call`, a browser) with their reason. Money is the only closed path: no tick reaches an order or a transfer. The defaults are every read plus web search; restricting has no upside beyond spend, so narrow a list only when the user asked.
- **The lines vary with the terms.** `Tools:` prints the list in the menu's words (`no tools` on `[]`; a narrower pick names its rows, a partial theme its count); `Reaches out:` names where the run leaves this home (`web + X search on this model`, `web search on this model`, `pages by address through OpenMarket's own fetcher`, `your files at OpenAI`, `images on xAI`) and what may travel with it, `nothing beyond this home` for reads alone; a ticked row the lane cannot run prints its capability row (`Make images: not on your lane (anthropic/claude-x: Anthropic has no image API). Connect OpenAI, xAI or Google in setup, or run without images.`) and the run proceeds without it, refusing `maker_tool_unsupported` at run time; `Answer: one answer a run` for `answer`; a cadence of a day or longer reads `up to N runs a month`; `(your cap: M)` prints only with `max_runs_per_day`. The credential gloss is `your home's Grok credential`, `your stored <provider> credential`, `your <provider> credential, the author's pick`, or nothing when the run rides the chat model's own. Never pass `model_authorization` yourself; the runtime strips it.
- **The pick.** `model` (`provider/model`; `--model` at the terminal) when the user names one: its provider must resolve on this home, else `model_credential_missing` names what to connect. Otherwise the author's `recommended_model` when this home holds that provider's credential, else the chat lane. The chat model never changes, and nobody is ever told to switch chat models: `model` picks another lane for this run alone, and the card says what the picked lane can reach.
- **What the lane can run.** `web_research` runs on the sealed lane and nowhere else: X reach exists only when that lane is xai (unattended, it never falls back to the home's Grok credential), web reach on a lane with hosted web search, none otherwise; files and images need an API key at OpenAI or Google (images also xAI), so an author who needs X recommends xai, and an installer on Anthropic reads the images capability row before saying yes.
- **The caps.** `max_tool_calls` per run, default 20, at most 32 (each search, file or image call is one more request on the user's own credential; the nested call's own tokens are not counted on the run row); `max_runs_per_day` (`--max-runs-per-day`), the person's number, counted per UTC day across scheduled, attended and probe runs, the rest holding until the day rolls over (absent, the cadence alone bounds the day); `every` at least 60s, and the cadence is the ceiling (the `Ceiling:` line states it); one run at a time per authority; a run ends at its deadline (`OM_DEADLINE_MODEL_SOURCE_RUN`, 600s by default) with no rows. Say "runs" and "calls", never a currency.
- **`goal`.** Absent, every finding counts (`accept_all`: no classifier, no credential beyond the run's). Present, the classifier judges every finding against it after the run (`llm_every_event`, every finding, no daily budget: §"Model use"). `name` names the source (default `Model run`); `notify` (default true, `--silent` at the terminal) pings the channel per finding. On a model source the goal and the delivery destination are sealed terms too: a change rides the same card.
- **The first read and the reply.** After the yes: the seal, then the watch is saved and enabled at once (`first_read: "pending"`, `opening_items: null`). The daemon's first read is the first run under the sealed terms (it counts as the day's first under the cap): its findings are stored as the source's opening items (never notified). The row, the sheet, `om watch list|show` and the background rail say what that read did in one phrase family: `first read pending` before the daemon starts, `first read · m:ss` while it runs, `opened with N items · HH:MM` or `opened empty · HH:MM` once the opening items are stored (until the next read, then `working`), `first read failed · <reason>` when the run failed (a broken row; a read now retries). The page and `om watch show` list the opening items under `Opening read · <time> · what already existed when the watch was born · not sent`; `opening_read` on `watch_show` carries the same facts. A finding may carry an `artifact_id` (an image the run made); the delivery attaches it. The reply is one outcome line: the model, the cadence, the group, then `first read pending, the row shows it` (`Added: a model run every 1 h on xai/grok-4.3 to <group>; first read pending, the row shows it`). `--probe-now` (`probe: "now"`) is the way to see the sample at once: one run before the save, its findings stored as the opening items, and the reply says how many it found with a sample title or two. `status: adopted` means the group already carries this exact run (same prompt, cadence, tools and answer shape) with a standing seal, and nothing new was created; `not_created` carries the reason (the seal refused, or with `--probe-now` the probe refused or failed): the seal was revoked and nothing was left behind, never a half-made, paused watch. A run that asked for a row it cannot use says so on its own receipt (the run row's `missing_tools`, printed on `om watch show`'s last-run line); with `--probe-now` the reply says it (`the probe asked for make_image; this run cannot use it; tick it on the menu or change the prompt`).
- **Read and change.** `watch_show` (`om watch show <slug>`) prints a `Model run` block: the lane and whose credential, the author's advice, the prompt whole, `tools:` in the menu's words with the cap, `reaches out:`, the answer shape, `ceiling:` with the daily cap and today's count, and the last run (when, status, findings, tool calls, summary); `watch_history` reads its rows like any source's. The prompt is yours after the install: `watch_edit` with any sealed term (`prompt`, `tools`, `output`, `max_tool_calls`, `max_runs_per_day`, `model`; `om watch edit <slug> --prompt|--tools|--output|--max-tool-calls|--max-runs-per-day|--model`) raises ONE never-auto card over the merged resulting terms, every changed value printed whole (old and new), and re-seals on the yes; the stream and the journal are untouched; a source that landed unsealed (an import) is sealed here; any other watch refuses `watching_model_not_a_model_source`; MCP refuses. `take_source` (`om watch source take <slug>`) restores the author's shipped terms on the same card. A run that cannot dial stands the source down with its reason: `dead` on `authority_missing` or `authority_revoked` (seal it with `om watch edit <slug> --model`), `degraded` on `credential_missing` (connect the sealed provider), `model_source_disabled` (`om config set agent.model_source on`), a provider error or the deadline; `cap_reached` holds the run until the UTC day rolls over and says so.
- **Refusals.** `model_authorization_required`: no card came back; in om chat the card asks, at a terminal `om watch model-add` confirms, MCP cannot create one. `model_authorization_drift`: the call would seal different terms than the card froze (the prompt, cadence, tools, output, caps, model, image lane, store or reach); approve again so the card shows the current terms. `model_authorization_replayed`: an approval seals one run; approve again. `watching_unknown_tool`: a name outside the menu; the nearest callable rides the message: name it or drop it. `maker_tool_unsupported`: a ticked row the sealed lane cannot run, refused at run time with the fix; `image_lane_unsealed` / `file_store_not_sealed`: a call for a row the approval did not tick. `model_credential_missing`: the named provider, or any lane at all, has no credential on this home; the hint names what to connect (`/setup`, `om init`, or the env key), and the chat model stays. `model_cadence_invalid`: not a duration, or under 60s. `model_daily_cap_invalid`: `max_runs_per_day` is not a whole number of at least 1.
- **Sharing.** The recipe carries the prompt, `tools` as authored (absent, `[]` or the list), the answer shape, the caps and the author's recommendation (`recommended_model`: provider, model, one line why); it never carries the sealed authority (`extra.auth`), the installer's pick, the image lane, the store or a credential. Every home seals its own where a human says yes: the install card's `Model:` line names the pick (`--model` picks another, an update keeps it), its `Runs:` and `Reaches out:` lines the resolved terms on that lane, and any capability row; an update prints `Changed:` rows for every moved term and `Customized:` for one you edited; `watch_import` lands the source unsealed and its receipt names `om watch edit <slug> --model provider/model` as the seal; MCP refuses a pack that ships one. The install side: `skill_read("marketplace", section = "Watch packs")`.

## Timer source

A timer fires the steps on a cadence or at an instant; read this for "every morning do X" and "once at 4pm".

The arm is complete in the `watch_create` schema (the intent `"timer_create"` needs no `schema_read`). From chat the door opens only through the composer's timer handoff: its `next` entry carries the cron and the zone (or the instant), the handoff's build says what the step reads and whether it may search the web, and a call in any other shape is refused.

`{kind: "timer", cron: "0 8 * * 1-5", tz: "America/New_York"}` fires every occurrence from creation forward, staggered up to five minutes on hourly-or-longer periods (the show surface reports the effective instant); `{kind: "timer", at: "<ISO>"}` fires once within a 120s grace of the instant and retires. A timer has no rows of its own beyond the occurrences; its history holds the steps' run rows. A timer has no `act_within` fence unless the watch names one (a coalesced weekend occurrence still runs its bookkeeping steps); a money step on it still moves money only within its own `act_within` (default 5m, measured from the staggered due instant), else its occurrence settles `expired` while its sibling steps run. The CLI spelling is `om watch create <label> --every "<cron>" [--tz <zone>]` or `--at <instant>` with `--ai <prompt>`, `--tool <action> --args <json>` or `--screenshot <workspace-id>`.

## Upstream source

An upstream watch runs its steps on the live rows of other watches; read this for "when watch Z lands, do X", a step over a folder, digests, and one watch with several sources.

The arm is complete in the `watch_create` schema (the intent `"upstream_create"` needs no `schema_read`).

`{kind: "upstream", watch_ids: ["<id or slug>", ...]}` (1 to 100, unique) binds to each named watch's journal from the binding's floor forward: a row from ANY of them is a row here, in (producer commit, row) order, and the row keeps its producer's slug in `context.upstream` so the steps and `om watch show` can say which source fired. Only source rows cross; a step's result rows never do, and backfill or catch-up rows never fire it. Cycles, a ninth link and a producer past the cap refuse typed (`watching_upstream_cycle`, `watching_upstream_too_deep`, `watching_unknown_upstream`); a producer removed later stands the watch down with `journal_source_missing` until it is back, and the watch's row reads `source broken · the watch it reads from is gone` with `om watch edit <reader>` as the fix (point it at another source, or add the source again under the same id; `om watch remove <reader>` clears a reader nobody wants); a reader in that state never blocks another watch's save (§"Errors", `roster_warnings`). One quiet window applies (the watch's notify `min_interval_sec`): inside it the first committed row of a burst wins. The CLI spelling is `--upstream <watch>` (repeatable).

One watch with several sources: `watch_create` with `sources[]` (each `condition:<json>`, `every:<cadence>`, `at:<instant>`, `upstream:<watch>`, `stream-ref:<json>` or `inbound`; the CLI spelling is `--source <kind>:<value>`, repeatable) writes ONE file: one id, one name, one history, one status, one notify, and every entry keeps its own goal and classifier. `watch_source_add` appends an entry to a saved watch (`source`) or a whole list of them (`sources[]`, up to 100 in one call: pass every item the user named in one call, one card grouped by kind covers them all, never loop one item per call; a list that would pass 100 sources refuses whole naming the count, and an entry the watch cannot take comes back as a skipped row with its reason while the rest land; a script condition goes alone through `source`), and `watch_source_remove` drops one; the reply names every entry added or skipped and the counts asked, added, skipped. `om watch list` prints `<label> · N sources · M chains · <state>`, and `om watch show` lists each entry by name with its own last fire, then the chains. Pause/resume with the watch id affects the whole watch; `source_enabled` on `watch_edit` (`om watch source pause <watch> <name>`) pauses one entry, and its `role` says whether it triggers the steps or only supplies context (§"Trigger and context roles"). A page, search or model source goes through its own door (`watch_page_add`, `watch_search_add`, `watch_model_add`) with `watch` naming the watch it joins.

A step has two scopes, and the folder page shows both. A step for one person goes on that person's watch (`watch_action_add`; the member's own page lists it, and the folder page keeps the step count in that member's row). A step over a folder is the folder's own watch: ONE `watch_create` with `group` naming the folder and `sources` naming every member as an `upstream` entry, the step in `actions`. To act when any member fires, the entries are `role: "trigger"` and nothing else; for a daily or hourly brief, one `timer` entry (`cron` and `tz`) is the trigger and every member entry is `role: "context"` (`context_stale_after` caps how old a member's newest row may be), so a member's fire never runs the brief and the timer runs it once over what the members fired. The prompt carries the person's ask alone: the members are the entries, never a list of names or slugs in the prompt, so a pack can carry the watch and a member that joins later is one more entry (`watch_source_add`). The folder page lists the folder's own watch under OVER ALL N (`daily at 18:00 ▸ notable stances ▸ discord-general`), out of the member list and the tab count, with the sends row for the members under EACH WATCH; `watch_compose` writes a brief on a clock with the combined watch as its context; a brief naming one source reads that source. A step on each combined update belongs on the combined watch itself. A money step rides it like any watch: one chain, one arm card, and an order may run per member fire the judge admits.

## Trigger and context roles

Each source on a watch either triggers it or only supplies context; read this before adding a second source, or a news feed beside a price rule.

`role` on each `sources[]` entry is `trigger` (the default: an accepted row is recorded and starts the watch's root steps and original-update delivery) or `context` (the source's accepted history stays readable by the steps, through the header every ai step receives and `watch_history({source, last})`, and starts nothing on this watch). Several triggers are OR: an accepted row from any one starts a run. A context source keeps its own collection, filter and budgets, and using another watch as context changes nothing about that watch. The role belongs to the relationship, not the source: the same feed is a trigger on one watch and context on another. A draft with only context sources cannot be enabled; offer a timer as the trigger. The card, `om watch show` and the header list each source with its role and freshness; context is fetched on demand, never pasted into every prompt. Changing a role keeps the source's identity and re-checks the chains and any published definition that read it. "News can inform the BTC decision without every headline starting one" is exactly this shape: the feed as context, the price rule as the trigger.

## Actions

The three step kinds, how they chain, and delivery as a setting; read this before adding, removing or explaining a step.

| Kind | Payload | Runs | Emits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ai | `{label, prompt, tools?, recommended_model?, max_runs_per_day?, budgetMs?, maxToolCalls?, output?}` | one headless turn per row (a verdict step re-run over the same row reads its first answer and takes no daily run): the sealed prompt as untrusted instructions, the row as a separate untrusted field, ONLY the sealed tool set (absent `tools` = every read plus web search, `[]` = none, a list = exactly those) | text (default), one word from a declared list, or a verdict (§"AI step") |
| tool | `{label, action, args}` | one unattended dispatch of a sealed action with frozen arguments (`chart_screenshot`, a read, `metric_rule`) | its result as text; `metric_rule` emits a verdict (§"Tool step") |
| money | `{label, mode, terms, boxes, caps}` | one movement of money through the invocation ledger: an order, a cancel, a strategy leg, a transfer, a purchase or a subscription cancel | the receipt (§"Money step") |

Every step has a name: `label` on the payload (1 to 60 chars, unique within the watch, case-insensitive; a repeat refuses `watching_duplicate_step_label`, the same class of refusal as a duplicate source name). The name is what every surface prints for the step, with the id beside it where a command needs it: the delivery header reads `<watch> · <step name>`, `om watch history` lists `step: <name>` in KIND (the id in `--format json`), `om watch show` and the share card list `<name> (<id>)`, the arm card opens each line with it, and the step-answer card in chat leads with it. Give every step a name when you author it (`--label <name>` beside `--ai`, `--tool`, `--money` or `--screenshot`; `payload.label` on `watch_action_add` and `watch_create`); a step left unnamed is named from what it does (a word-list ai step `dip or noise`, a verdict step `long/short/flat call`, a free-text step by the first five words of its prompt, a tool step `metric_get on BTCUSDT`, a money step `buy ETH 100` or `strategy on ETH`). Wherever a verb takes a step id, its name works too (`--after`, `--target`, `om watch action remove`, `om watch arm <watch> <step>`, `--box <step>.size=`, `--fires-when <step>:...`); a name several steps answer to refuses `watching_action_ambiguous` and the ids are the fix. `om watch action rename <watch> <step> "<new name>"` (`watch_action_rename`) changes the name and nothing else: an armed chain stays armed, because a name is never part of what the user approved. Names travel in recipes; ids stay as minted.

Delivery is not a step: `notify` on the watch (`telegram`, `discord`, `slack`, `email`, `webhook`) is where every step's result and every fire goes, and a step's `channel` overrides it. Either one takes a configured channel name or an address such as `discord:#ops`, `slack:C0123ABCD` or `telegram:-1001234567890:17585`, minted on first use. `webhook` is the universal outbound door (a JSON POST to any endpoint); the `inbound` source is the inbound door: together they connect a watch to anything.

Chaining: a step reads the watch's source rows by default; `input: {watch_id, action_id}` (`--after <step>` at the CLI fills the watch) makes it read one producer step's result rows instead, on the same watch or a sibling source of the same watch. One producer per step, fan-out from one producer to several readers is fine, no cycles, depth 8. A producer declares what it emits (`output`) and every reader's requirement is fixed by its mode (§"Money step"); a reader the producer cannot satisfy refuses `watching_reader_unsatisfied`, and one producer feeding readers with different requirements refuses `watching_fanout_incompatible`. A `cancel_order` money step names the order step it targets (`terms.target.step`, `--target <step>`), a reference edge that joins the two into one chain.

A chain is every step connected through `input` or a reference edge (steps that touch belong to one chain and arm together). A step may also `reads: {source?, field, kinds?}` one field of a pinned contract (a follow's, or a named upstream watch's when two sources carry the same name) in place of the row's raw text; the AI step gets the value as fenced data; the step runs only on posts of kinds carrying that field, and `kinds` (`only these kinds`) narrows it to the listed kind ids; a name no kind of the contract carries refuses `watching_reads_unknown_field`, a kind not on the menu refuses `watching_reads_unknown_kind`, a listed kind without the field refuses `watching_reads_kind_without_field`, two sources with the name and no `source` refuse `watching_reads_ambiguous_source`, and `reads` beside `input` refuses `watching_reads_with_input` (a step's answers carry no fields: drop one). Each step has an immutable `id` (minted when omitted), `enabled` (false until its chain is armed) and `origin` (`local`, or `package` when an install landed it). Every step lands disabled; the create or attach result carries `chains[].arm_command` (`om watch arm <watch> <chain>`), which is what you name (§"Arming"). An ai step runs on the default set: omitting `tools` seals every audited read plus web search as a concrete list; pass a list only when the user asked to narrow, `[]` for a reply-only step; tick `page_read`, `search_files` or `make_image` when the task needs them (each is one more request on the AI credential, and the card's `reaches out:` line says where the run goes).

The CLI: `om watch action add <watch> --label <name> --ai <prompt> [--output text|word:a,b,c|verdict] [--tools <names|none>] [--recommend provider/model:"reason"] [--max-runs-per-day <n>] [--image-model <id>] [--file-store <id>] | --tool <action> --args <json> | --money <mode> --terms <json> [--target <step>] | --screenshot <workspace>` with `--after <step>` and `--channel <name>` (`--help` prints the menu); `om watch action rename <watch> <step> "<new name>"` (the chain stays armed); `om watch action remove <watch> <step>` (by id or name; a producer with readers refuses `watching_action_in_use`, naming them). None of them arms anything: `om watch arm <watch> <chain>` does.

## AI step

One prompt over the rows it reads, emitting text, one word from a declared list, or a verdict; read this before authoring a step a money step or a channel reads.

`{kind: "ai", payload: {prompt, tools?, recommended_model?, max_runs_per_day?, budgetMs?, maxToolCalls?, output?}, input?}`. `tools` is the menu, the same field and rule as a model run's (§"Model source"): absent seals the default set (every read on this home plus web search), `[]` none, a list exactly those; `image_model`, `image_size`, `image_quality` and `file_store` are written by the home behind the images and files rows, never by a package. `recommended_model` is the author's advice for the chain's model (provider, model, one line why): the chain resolves ONE model first, the first ai step's recommendation when this home holds that credential, else the chat lane, and every step's rows are checked against it; a later step's advice prints as `ignored: step <id> recommends <provider/model>`. `max_runs_per_day` is the person's daily cap on the step's runs, counted per UTC day by the step's authority; the rest hold until the day rolls over. `output` declares what the step emits: `{type: "text"}` (default, delivered to the channel as a bounded reply or nothing), `{type: "word", words: [...]}` (exactly one lowercase word from the list, 1 to 16 words) or `{type: "verdict"}` (`{direction: long | short | flat, confidence: 0..1}`). The runner parses the answer strictly: a word outside the list or a malformed verdict is recorded as a typed `no_action` row and nothing downstream runs for that row. The list must cover what the reader needs: an `order` reads `buy | sell | none`, `cancel_order` and `cancel_subscription` read `cancel | skip`, `transfer` reads `send | skip`, `purchase` reads `buy | skip`, a `strategy` reads a verdict. The producer row reaches the model as a fenced untrusted source beside the sealed prompt; the model is the chain's resolved lane. The arm card opens on what needs vetting: `Reads` (where the run reaches outside this home, in plain nouns, with the largest step's tool-call cap), `Sends to`, `Cost` (the ai runs per fire, change or row, on whose account) and `Money` when any moves; the question names the steps and the root's trigger (`Run "read" and "order" on every fire of "desk"?`). `d` (Show details) re-draws the same card with the details tier, and every other surface prints it under `Details`: `TRIGGER` (the watch, a page's address and cadence, the filter), one `STEP` group per step (`Name`, `Model` with the step's own `up to N tool calls a run`, `Tools` in the menu's words (`everything OpenMarket can read (incl. your positions and balances), Search the web`; `no tools` on `[]`), `Author recommends`, `Daily cap`, one `⚠` row per capability the lane cannot run (`Make images: not on your lane (...). Connect OpenAI, xAI or Google in setup, or arm without images.`) and one `⚠` row where the tools reach outside this home), and one `INSTRUCTIONS` group per ai step with the prompt whole, one sentence per line, on every surface; a changed concrete list stands an armed chain down and the re-arm card prints the new rows.

```json
{
  "id": "judge-1",
  "kind": "ai",
  "payload": {
    "prompt": "Read the fire. Is this dip a buy, a sell, or nothing?",
    "output": { "type": "word", "words": ["buy", "sell", "none"] }
  }
}
```

## Mentions

Sources are named, and a prompt names one with `@{Name}`; read this before writing a source's name into any ai step.

Every source on a watch has a unique name: an authored `name` on the source (`names` per sibling on an upstream source), else one derived from what it watches (a condition `BINANCE_FUTURES:BTCUSDT rule`, an X handle `@DeItaone`, a page its host, a feed its display name or host, an inbound door the watch's label, a timer `timer`; on a watch with several sources each one by its slug); a derived repeat gains ` (2)`, an authored duplicate refuses `watching_duplicate_source_name`. In a prompt or a `definitions` field write `@{Name}` exactly as `watch_show` prints it (a single-token name may be bare: `@DeItaone`): the model sees the name and the header says what it is. A name the watch does not have refuses at create with the names that exist (`watching_unknown_source`); a function the menu does not carry refuses with the nearest callable (`watching_unknown_tool`). So read the watch first (`watch_show`), read the menu (`watch_tools`), then write. What the step sees, the menu with return shapes, the answer shapes, the `definitions` fields for a decision or a word list, and worked prompts: `skill_read("watch-prompts")`.

## Tool step

One fixed function with frozen arguments, dispatched unattended; read this for screenshots, reads on a cadence and rule steps.

`{kind: "tool", payload: {action, args, label?}, input?}` seals one audited action and its exact arguments; the card states the whole call. `chart_screenshot` renders a workspace and the image rides the delivery as an attachment (`--screenshot <workspace> [--caption]` is the sugar for it). Local destinations receive the same rendered image; a chart cannot be selected as a live public output until attachment transport is supported. `metric_rule` is a rule step: it emits a verdict a strategy money step can read without a model. A tool step never names a money function (`order_place`, `wallet_send`): money moves through a money step and its ledger only.

For a deterministic verdict, set `payload.action: "metric_rule"` and `args: {rules: [{condition: {metric: "rsi", selector: {exchange: "BINANCE_FUTURES", symbol: "BTCUSDT", interval: "HOUR"}, params: {period: 14}, op: "lt", value: 30}, direction: "long", confidence: 0.8}]}`. Rules run in order; the first match wins, omitted confidence is 1, and no match returns `{direction: "flat", confidence: 0}`. Missing required data refuses `metric_rule_unavailable`. Conditions support built-in metrics, `all`/`any`/`not` and crossings, with at most 16 rules and 8 distinct operands; scripts, journal events and WRUN are refused. The verdict can feed a money strategy or a selected public step output, including its own answer gate.

## Money step

The six money modes, their terms, human-entered boxes and caps; read this before authoring a step that moves money.

The money step: six modes, the terms an author fixes, the boxes the human types at the arm, the caps; read this before authoring anything that moves money. The full arm, every mode's fields with their shapes: `schema_read {intent: "money_step"}` (the ai and tool arms are complete in the tool schema).

`{kind: "money", payload: {mode, terms, boxes, caps}, input?}`. The author fixes the STRUCTURE in `terms`; the AMOUNTS are boxes the human types on the arm card, and the recipe carries at most a `hint` per box (a `value` inside a recipe refuses `watching_box_value_refused`). `caps` is `{max_fires (default 1), max_size?, expires_at?}` on every mode but strategy. `order` and `strategy` may carry `act_within` beside `caps`: the step's own late fence, narrowing the watch's only (default the smaller of the watch's and 5m; wider refuses `wider than the watch's <v>; raise the watch's first`); a row older than it moves no money and the step's run settles `expired`. With a producer (`input`), its word decides whether the step runs on that row; without one, the source row itself fires it — except an input-less `strategy`, which reads its condition source's evaluations rather than its fires (§"Strategy mode").

| Mode | `terms` | Boxes | Reads from the producer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `order` | `{venue: "hyperliquid", asset, market?, side?, order_type, limit_px?, slippage?, reduce_only?, brackets?, size_mode}` or `{venue: "polymarket", market, side?, order_type, limit_price?, worst_price?, size_mode}` | `size` | `buy / sell / none` names the side (a sealed `terms.side` stands; `none` does nothing); no producer needs `terms.side` |
| `cancel_order` | `{venue, target: {step: "<order step id>"}}` | none | `cancel / skip`; fires only on rows after the targeted order's receipt, cancelling that root's own order |
| `strategy` | `{market, sizer: {config, scale, sides?, min_confidence?, leverage?}, exit?, stop_breaker?, entry_freshness?, slippage?, wake?}` (plus `on_true`, `on_false?`, `entry_cooldown?` with no input) | `capital` | a verdict: `long / short / flat` with confidence — or, with no input on a condition watch, that source's level (§"Strategy mode") |
| `transfer` | `{asset: "SOL" / "USDC"}` | `to`, `amount` | `send / skip` |
| `purchase` | `{listing: "@scope/name", mint?}` | `price` | `buy / skip`; the quote is re-read at fire and drift refuses |
| `cancel_subscription` | `{listing}` | `price` | `cancel / skip` |

An `order` fires a real order on the paired venue on every fire until `max_fires` is spent; `reduce_only` is the close-only tool and repeated fires stack exposure unless capped; a venue with no paired account arms all the same and discloses `venue_note`. `cancel_order` may remove a protective stop: the card says so. `transfer`, `purchase` and `cancel_subscription` move wallet money on a signal under the same caps and the same arm ritual. Every autonomous dispatch carries one ledger key (chain epoch, root row, step, leg): a replay of the same row is a `duplicate` and nothing is sent twice; an unknown outcome reconciles at boot and never resends.

## Strategy mode

The strategy money step: a pinned market, a sizer with side multipliers and managed exits, fed by a verdict or a condition level; read before creating or arming a trading rule.

`{kind: "money", input?: {watch_id, action_id: "<verdict producer>"}, payload: {mode: "strategy", terms: {market, sizer, exit?, stop_breaker?, entry_freshness?, slippage?, wake?, cohort_crash_policy?, on_true?, on_false?, entry_cooldown?}, boxes: {capital: {hint}}, caps?, act_within?}}`. `market` pins `{venue: "polymarket", condition_id, long_outcome}`, `{venue: "hyperliquid", coin}` or `{venue: "market_data", exchange, symbol}` (paper only). `sizer` is `config.mode` (`conviction` with `on_neutral` / `on_reversal` / `flip_threshold`, `always_in`, or `single_sided` with `side`), `scale` (`fixed` or `conviction`), `sides` (`{long: 1.0, short: 0.5}` multipliers, missing side = 1), `leverage` (perps only) and `min_confidence`; the `capital` box (`{source: "fixed", amount}`, `wallet`, or `{source: "fraction_of_wallet", fraction}`) is typed at the arm. A leg is sized capital x side multiplier x leverage x confidence, bounded by `caps.max_size` (a level reader has no confidence of its own and enters at 1, so `scale: "conviction"` does nothing there); a strategy has no `max_fires` (one position, resized on every verdict — on every level move for a level reader). `exit` carries `bracket` (`tp`, `sl` as P&L fractions or `tp_price` / `sl_price`) and a `time_stop`, enforced by the daemon while the chain is armed; a close-only leg of a position the chain opened still dispatches after the chain stood down. The producer is an ai step declaring `verdict` or a `metric_rule` tool step (`watching_strategy_needs_verdict` otherwise) — unless the watch carries exactly one `condition` source, where the step may carry NO `input` and trade that source's level: `terms.on_true` (required, the side a true level takes), `terms.on_false` (absent reads as flat), `terms.entry_cooldown` (the least time between this strategy acting and its next entry, on `live` only, never shorter than the 60s floor a refusing venue earns). Those three are refused on a step that reads a verdict, `goal` is refused on the condition beside such a step, a second condition source is refused, and such a watch already carrying a step id'd `source` is refused (`watching_reserved_action_id`). The lane holds its position, running the managed exits, while the source is not definite, while its newest evaluation is older than `entry_freshness.max_event_age_secs` (else 5m), or while that evaluation is stamped ahead of this machine's clock; a protective or venue-side close blocks re-entry until the level moves. `run_mode` is the rung the arm stamps: `paper` (walletless simulated account: a persistent book takes simulated fills, no venue order; the default), `dry_run` (wallet-backed reads: the real account and holding size the plan, no venue order) or `live` (wallet-backed: real orders through the capped execution path); live and dry_run are reached only by an explicit mode on the arm card, with the terminal flags `--allow-same-market`, `--allow-unverified-cohort`, `--allow-existing-position`. `wake.mode` runs one agent turn after a managed exit: `propose` records a thesis rewrite, `autonomous` is standing authority to apply it and cards. Reads: `watch_execute_history`, `watch_execute_digest` (its `schedule_set` mode stands a daily edition; `watch_execute_digest_run` writes one now); `watch_execute_paper_reset` is the one destroyer of a paper record.

For a backtested condition-source candidate, the terminal accepts
`om watch create "<label>" --from-candidate winner.json --deliver <channel> --format json`.
It preserves closed-bar evaluation, bands and source options, and copies the
supported strategy terms into one money step. The backtest capital becomes
`boxes.capital.hint`; a recipe cannot supply a box value. Market and sizer are
required. Fixed-view, watch, signal-only, daemon and label arms refuse.
An exit with `track_target: false` also refuses because the step cannot retain it.
The receipt carries `slug`, `actions[].id` for the money step, and
`chains[].chain_id` with that step in `action_ids`. JSON or non-TTY creation
never prompts to arm. Use those ids for the separate terminal paper arm in
research.md §"Candidates and promotion".

## Arming

One card per chain, one yes arms every step of it, money chains arm at the terminal, and a drift stands the chain down; read this before saying anything is armed.

A chain is armed by `watch_arm {watch, chain_id}` (`om watch arm <watch> <chain>`; `<chain>` is the chain id, an unambiguous prefix, or any of its step ids). The card lists every step in producer order: an ai step's prompt (whole on the terminal, first sentence on chat with the full prompt behind an expand) and its model; a tool step's function and frozen arguments; a money step's line, whole and never collapsed (mode, venue or address, asset, every box as `<box>: <value>` or `<box>: <hint>`, the fire cap, per-fire and aggregate exposure, sides and leverage, the full recipient or cancel target, "renews until cancelled", the stop-removal warning); then `deliver <channel>`, the cadence, and `yes arms all N · no leaves the chain off`. Boxes are typed on the terminal card, one prompt per box with the recipe's hint beside it (`--box <step>.<box>=<value>` from the shell); the values are sealed in the same transaction as the epoch. A no writes nothing and answers `chain off: you declined <step>` with `Arm it later: om watch arm <watch> <chain>`. Chains of one watch arm independently; deliver needs no arm; `watch_resume` re-arms nothing.

Surfaces: the chat card arms a chain with NO money step (`armed at chat`). A chain with a money step in chat previews the card and returns the terminal command (`money_needs_terminal`), consuming nothing; run it on the installing machine. MCP refuses every chain arm (`mcp_cannot_arm_chain`), capital window or not. The arm state you read back is one of `armed at terminal`, `armed at chat`, `off`, `declined at <step>`, `stood down: <reason>`, `blocked by in-flight <invocation>`.

The yes is digested over everything the chain depends on: the source, every step's definition (prompt, output, tool arguments, money terms), every box value, every cap, every dependency pin, the delivery channel and the producers' generations. Any change to any of them (an edit, a package update, a hand edit of the file) stands the chain down at its next firing (`chain_digest_drift`) with the reason on `watch_show`, and `om watch arm` asks again. A box edit (`watch_edit` with `boxes`) stands the chain down at once and the card comes back with the new value already typed. Re-arming while a money invocation is still settling refuses `arm_blocked_by_inflight` naming it; reconcile or wait, then arm again. Pause deactivates every chain of the watch; remove tombstones the steps for good.

A schedule file an older om sealed under `toolsAllow` (the key renamed to `tools`) reads with NO tool list: the step reads `waiting for your OK`, nothing dials, the daemon names the file once per boot (`[schedule] <watch>--<step>.json carries the older toolsAllow key: the step waits for your OK; arm it again: om watch arm <watch> <step> (or press a in /watches)`), and a chain armed on it stands down before any model call (`tool scope refused: the seal wrote no tool list onto this firing's file; arm the chain again so the card prints the list it seals`, chip `needs you`). The one recovery is `watch_arm`: the card prints today's tool scope and the yes writes it; the read rewrites nothing.

## Read a watch

The read verbs: list, show, history, stats, state, the overview; read this before answering what a watch is doing, what is armed or why it is quiet.

For notifications across the whole home, use `notis_list` (`om notis`, or `/notis` in chat), then `notis_get` for a full saved message. Fires, follow arrivals and daemon notices are recorded even without a messenger. History belongs to the connected daemon; an unavailable read is not proof that nothing happened.

- `watch_list` (`om watch list`): one row per watch the user made (`<label> · N sources · M chains · <state>`), then any `unreadable` rows with their removal command; `group` and `kind` filters.
- `watch_show` (`om watch show <id>`): the sources as rows with their own last fire and what their rows arrive as, the chains with every step's one line and its arm state (`arm: om watch arm <watch> <chain>` while off), the boxes with `edit boxes: <command>`, the channel with `change channel: <command>`, then the stored details. A source's own card names the watch it belongs to.
- `watch_history` (`om watch history <id>`): the merged ledger newest first: source rows, step result rows (`result_kind: text | word | verdict`, `no_action` rows, error rows) and money receipts, with `kind: "fired" | "error"` filters and a window. Use it before speculating about a missed delivery.
- `watch_stats` (`om watch stats [id]`): fires, late fires, per-channel delivery, detected gaps, `condition_text`, `last_error` and `repair`; counts are floors when `data_complete` is false, never an uptime percentage.
- `watch_state_show` / `watch_state_clear`: a script condition's persisted state.
- `watch_journal_stats`, `event_journal_list` / `event_journal_get` / `event_journal_search`: the Markdown journal a listener writes (`events.md`, `overview.md`), on-demand context only.
- `watching_overview`: one roster of every watch with its chains, attention items (paused, erroring, waiting for an arm, stood down, blocked) and the daemon's state. Render it as rows, never as counts.

## Edit a watch

`watch_edit` patches one watch in place; read this before changing a goal, a condition, a classifier, a destination, a box or the step roster.

`watch_edit` (`om watch edit <id>`) takes any envelope field, a replacement `source` (a condition rewrite re-arms the fire state), `user_goal` / `classifier` / `notify` / `group` / `related_markets`, `boxes` (`{"<step>.<box>": value}`: the value is written onto the money step, the chain stands down and the arm card comes back with it typed), and on a model source the sealed terms (`prompt`, `tools`, `output`, `max_tool_calls`, `max_runs_per_day`, `model`, or `take_source` for the author's version back) under one never-auto card over the merged terms (§"Model source"), and on a search source its sealed terms (`subject`, `aliases`, `engine`, `cadence_sec`, `max_calls`, `window_days`, `intent`; `om watch edit <watch> --subject|--alias|--engine|--every|--max-calls|--window-days|--intent`, `source_name` / `source_id` naming the entry on a watch with several) under one never-auto card that prints the replacement's rows and a Changed row per moved term: the yes seals a replacement authority on the SAME watch (its id, group, journal and rows kept; the daemon's first read on the new terms stores its opening items) and retires the old authority after the save; a cadence-only change (`cadence_sec` alone) is the exception on every kind: it moves the standing authority's cadence in place (no replacement, nothing re-read or re-run, the stream, cursor and rows kept, the next read due at the new cadence; `search_reseal.in_place`, `model_reseal.next_check`), and on a page source `cadence_sec` needs no card at all (`page_cadence`); a changed subject drops the stored name variants unless `aliases` names new ones; unchanged terms mint nothing (`search_reseal.unchanged`); a search sealed by an older om carries no stored subject and refuses `search_terms_unsealed` until `subject` is set again; a search term beside any other field refuses `watching_search_edit_mixed`; MCP refuses. Steps are not edited here: `watch_action_add` replaces one in place by id and `watch_action_remove` detaches one; either stands the touched chain down until `om watch arm`. On a package-installed watch, replacing a step the package SHIPPED makes it yours: the result's `detached` says "this step shipped with <package>; your version is kept across updates" (no fork needed), an update keeps yours and says when the author changed theirs, and `watch_edit` with `take: ["<step>"]` (`om watch action take <watch> <step>`) puts the author's current version back, disabled, its chain re-asking. `watch_reclassify` re-runs only the rows whose first judgement failed (`error` rows: a missing credential, a provider outage); a changed goal or filter applies from the next read, and `watch_tune` previews it over this week's rows (§"Tune a watch"); `watch_amend` publishes a signed correction on a shared watch; `watch_synthesize` rewrites the overview now.

A listener's stream binding (the feed, the page, the X handle) is its identity and is never edited: create a new watch to read something else. How an X watch reads X is not on the watch either: OM picks the lane for every X watch at once from the home's X API key (saved and able to read: X's own API, official, billed per post read; otherwise the free public mirror). `watch_show`'s `x_coverage.summary` says which lane answered the last poll and `x_coverage.next` says the one door or fix (`om setup x-api` / the `/setup` X API row moves every X watch on its next poll; an API refusal names its own fix); "use my X API for this watch" is that door, never an edit and never a new watch.

For a vendor-backed news source, `watch_edit` takes `alert_when` (a Fast trigger), `interest` (a Topic subject) or `feed_label` (the vendor feed's name). Select one source with `source_id` or `source_name` when a watch has several. A trigger is a full replacement, not a fragment to append; `replace: true` acknowledges a confirmed replacement. A trigger change can discard replayed history and raises a confirmation card. The watch's `label` is its local name; `feed_label` changes the vendor name. The CLI spells these fields `--alert-when`, `--interest`, `--feed-label`, `--source-id`, `--source-name` and `--replace`. Delivery uses the watch's `notify`: `om watch edit <id> --notify-channel <name>` chooses a channel; `--clear-notify-channel` explicitly keeps it card-only, with no channel push. A folder's sends are set on every member at once: `watch_edit` with `select_group` naming the folder and the `notify` change (`om watch edit --group "<folder>" --notify-channel <name>`, `--add-notify-channel`, `--remove-notify-channel`, `--clear-notify-channel`), the folder page's sends row under EACH WATCH; the receipt names each member as saved or not saved with its reason. A channel-only change keeps each member's cadence, mute and style, a cadence-only change keeps its routing, a paused member takes the change without resuming, and the folder's own watch (the one that reads the members) is never in the cohort. Any other field with `select_group` is refused; `group` still moves ONE watch into a folder.

## Tune a watch

A complaint about the feed ("no rankings", "less fluff", "not this") is a filter tune: the person's words become `I want` / `ignore` lines on the right sources.

The watch's own judge re-reads this week's rows under them as a preview; the yes saves. Read this before answering a complaint about what a watch keeps or drops; `watch_tune {brief: true}` returns it.

1. The complaint is what the person said, in their words. Never rewrite the watch sentence (`user_goal`, `request`): the lines sit beside it.
2. Which sources. `watch_tune` with the watch (`id_or_slug`; a combined watch tunes the watches inside) or the folder (`group`) and no `sources` returns the flat list. The words decide the reach: a named watch → its sources; a named kind of source ("Chinese labs") → the sources of that kind, told apart by name, address and recent titles; a pointed row (`n`, `k`, `t`, or `rows`) → the row is the example, the reach still comes from the words; nothing named → every source of what they are looking at. Unclear words ("fluff") go everywhere, and the reply says how the word was read ("my reading of fluff, say otherwise").
3. The lines, in the person's words, one per want or no-no, worded for the source they land on. Pass the FULL `include` / `exclude` list per source after the change: the stored lines plus the new ones, a line the new one contradicts removed, never a delta.
4. `watch_tune` with `sources` runs the preview (paid model calls on the person's key, this week's newest 100 rows across the touched sources) and returns the card; a source that had no filter is `save: "save"`. Show it verbatim, as below; relay each `refused[].line`.
5. Yes = `watch_tune_apply {preview_id, fingerprint}`: its card asks `Update the filter?` (`Save the filter?` when every touched source had none), saves the lines per source and moves this week's flipped rows on LATEST; nothing is re-sent, a row that comes back is shown, never sent. Anything else is another complaint: `watch_tune` again with `refine_of` and the changed lines (only those sources run again).
6. On a watch still quiet until all good (§"Watch a subject"), re-ask `all good?` after the apply: `y start sending to <channel>` / `t tune again`.

```
Google News: AI models
  I want    a new architecture or open weights from a Chinese lab
            a release or a price change from a US lab
  ignore    rankings, listicles, weekly roundups
  this week 9 kept → 5 · 5 dropped · 1 back in
14 kept → 9 · 6 dropped · 1 back in · 4 model calls · nothing sent, nothing saved yet
12 followers receive what this watch keeps · they get the change too
⚠ Jiqizhixin keeps nothing this week
Update the filter?
```

The answers are `1. Yes (y / enter)`, `2. No, tell the agent what to do instead`, `d. Show only what changed` (the flipped rows with their reason, `already queued or sent` where a channel has one). The followers line appears on a live-shared watch (a follow relays what it keeps; an install is sealed); the red line when a source would keep nothing. A follow, a price, timer or page rule and a combined watch's own reader are refused with the line to relay; `tune_preview_gone` means run `watch_tune` again; on the apply, `filter_changed_meanwhile` refuses the one source whose lines moved while the others save, and `rows_pending` means the same apply again finishes it.

## Lifecycle

Pause, resume, remove, repair, the token verbs and the unreadable row; read this before stopping, restoring or deleting a watch.

- `watch_pause` / `watch_resume` (`om watch pause|resume <id>`): a paused watch produces no rows and fires nothing; its own strategy step is hands-off, exits included (resting native brackets keep enforcing venue-side), while strategy steps on other watches reading its rows keep running with their managed exits enforced; every chain waits for its own `om watch arm` after a resume; a resume that returns a running strategy step to operation restarts its stop-breaker counters, and a tripped step comes back only with its arm. One id changes one watch. `ids` names the exact set; `group` changes every watch under the named label. Both batch forms use one card. A combined watch's id selects its set: pause and remove keep sources another watch still reads and name them on the card; resume includes the set.
- `watch_remove` (`om watch remove <id>`): permanent. Drops the spec, the stored rows, snapshots, the chart binding, room shares and every chain's authority; the journal survives with its slug reserved. A watch with sources removes every source and says so. Cards on the lane, confirms on the CLI. Removing a page, search or model watch stands its sealed authority down (the row reads `revoked`): nothing dials on it again, and a re-add seals a fresh one on a fresh card. Every deletion logs `[watching] removed <id> (<cause>, <site>)` once in `runner.log` (the cause is the verb or flow: `watch_remove`, `watch_remove --force`, `panel remove`, `form discard`, `package update`, `package uninstall`, `unfollow`, `news retire`, `om reset`; the site `spec store`, `unreadable file`, `mutation kernel` or `clear all`).
  The removal is PLANNED before anything is destroyed, whole (one watch, a group, `ids`, a package uninstall): a reader left with no leg goes with it (recursively), a reader that keeps other legs is repaired (the id and its `names`/`roles` entries dropped through the edit door), a survivor that would break is paused with a note (`mentions @{<name>}, which is gone: edit the step or remove the reader`, the prompt never rewritten; `its remaining sources are all context: set one to trigger, or remove the reader`), an armed chain a repair moves waits for the OK again, a pack member detaches or takes its install's last-object consequence, and an unreadable watch file never holds. RESTRICT is judged against the FINAL surviving set: only a step on a surviving watch holds (`watch_has_consumers`, `watch '<slug>' not removed: <reason>.`; `force` severs it), a reader that goes with the leg never holds it, and a kept reader keeps its legs (`kept for <reader>, which reads it and stays (a reader keeps its sources)`). The card lists the plan under the consequence before the yes (`Also removed: <slug> (reads only <legs>, which goes)`, `Repaired: <slug> drops <names>; keeps <ids>`, `Paused: <slug>: <note>`, `Blocked by: ...; kept without --force`, `Kept: <leg>: kept for <reader>, which reads it and stays (a reader keeps its sources)`, `Needs your OK again: om watch arm <watch> <chain>`, `Package: <note>`, `Unreadable: N watch file(s) (<ids>); not a hold, named in the receipt`); relay these rows, never a bare count. The result's `cascade` (every form: the single receipt, the group and `ids` reports, `package_uninstall`) says what happened: `removed` (`via`, `lost`, `substeps`, `resumed`), `repaired` (`dropped`, `remaining`, `chains_to_arm`), `paused`, `skipped_restricted` (`reason`, `kept_for`), `unreadable`, `rearm`, `package_consequences`, `pending`, `failed`; non-empty `skipped_restricted` or `pending` means PARTIAL, say so. Each watch walks `remote`, `cleanup`, `spec`, `artifacts`; a failure (`watch_remove_failed`, `removing watch '<slug>' failed at <substep>: <error>`) stops the rest, and the one recovery is the same `watch_remove` again: it finishes from the substep that failed (`resumed: true`). A watch re-created since the plan refuses `watch_replaced`. A group, `ids` or marked-set uninstall is one operation: a member the cascade already took is never a failure, and a package the cascade removed whole never reports `not_installed`. `watch_unfollow` runs the same plan for the follow watch's readers.
- `watch_repair` (`om watch repair <watch>`): the `r` key on a watch whose files broke. Writes back from the spec only a step file that is gone or that this om cannot read; a file a newer om wrote or another copy's is refused and left as written (`refused` names the fix: `om upgrade`, or the removal); clears the faults that resolved (`cleared`); re-arms only the chains that were RUNNING before the fault (`chains_to_arm`: a terminal gives one without money its yes and asks the card for one with money; from chat call `watch_arm` with `chains_to_arm[0]`), while a chain that already waited for the person's OK keeps waiting and the status says `a`. A condition watch's evaluation health rides `condition`, and `rearm: true` (`--rearm`) re-arms its evaluation.
- `watch_retry` (`om watch retry <watch>`): after a fault, releases pending unowned classifier rows immediately, clears the engine's stop on the steps, writes back a missing step file and reports the status; it never gives an OK (a step stopped while running unattended waits for `watch_arm`).

## Model use

What a listener's reads cost and why rows stopped; read this before explaining a stall.

One control: the judge model (`agent.judge_model`, one home-wide pick) and the user's own provider key. There is no daily read budget on a watch, no page read cap and no backfill call budget: every admitted row is judged (one call judges a batch), every page change is read once. A search's lookup allowance (`search.max_calls`, default 5 a request) is a settable default the card discloses: a bound on the wire where the lane honors one (an OpenAI API key, xAI web search), an ask of the model where none exists (the ChatGPT/Codex backend, xAI's X search); the run discloses `searches used: N` either way. Under load the classifier parks the batches past its in-memory room in the intake ledger and reads them as room frees; nothing is dropped. A provider limit (429, quota) is a judge fault in words on `watch_show` (`the model refused: <provider> ... · fix: /setup judge model`; `the account's usage cap is reached on <provider>: re-judged when it lifts`) and the rows are re-judged on the provider's timing. `Model reads today: N` is a count, never a cap. `daily_cap` on an inbound source (default 2000) is the pusher's contract at the door and stays. `accept_all` runs no classifier and needs no credential.
